## Evidence rules

Record the exact candidate source SHA, host/platform, artifact digest, isolated instance IDs, transport, command/test, timestamp, exit/status, expected and observed results, sanitized evidence location and required approvals. Use PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or NOT VERIFIED per case. Do not infer provider traffic from a synthetic client or public ingress from a local socket. Keep private paths, raw provider requests, credentials and private audit keys out of this ledger.
